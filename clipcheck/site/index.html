ClipCheck

Catches every send

Send button and Ctrl/Cmd+Enter alike. If your draft says "see attached", "enclosed", or "my CV" and nothing's attached, you'll know before it leaves.

7 languages built in

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese phrases — "anbei", "ci-joint", "adjunto" and friends are all covered.

Smart, not naggy

No warning if you attached a file, linked to Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer, or if "see attached" only appears in the email you're replying to.

Private by design

Everything runs locally in your browser. Your emails are never sent to a server, never stored, never analyzed anywhere but on your machine.

Frequently asked questions

Does ClipCheck read my email?

Only locally, at the moment you press Send, to check for attachment phrases — the same way spell-check reads what you type. Nothing is transmitted or stored.

Is it free?

Yes — the full attachment check is free forever. A Pro upgrade adds your own custom keyword list (e.g. "the deck", "signed contract").

Does it work with Google Workspace accounts?

Yes. ClipCheck works on mail.google.com with any account, personal or Workspace.

What about Outlook?

Gmail only for now. Outlook has a similar feature built in; Gmail doesn't — that's why ClipCheck exists.
